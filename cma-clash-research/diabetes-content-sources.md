# Diabetes content sources

Seeded topic `ccma-diabetes` (NHA CCMA) and `phleb-glucose` (phlebotomy POC/specimens).

Primary references:

- American Diabetes Association Standards of Care in Diabetes (2025 supplement in Diabetes Care).
- ADA Classification and Diagnosis of Diabetes (Standards supplement).
- CDC diabetes basics and about pages.
- FDA CLIA waived tests overview.
- CLSI GP41 for venous specimen collection (phlebotomy glucose draws).

Quiz stems and study summaries were checked against these sources for diagnostic thresholds, hypoglycemia treatment, and scope-of-practice boundaries.
