# Plausibility pass — rewritten phlebotomy banks

Summaries of fact-check and distractor plausibility work on hand-authored seed banks.

## Phleb order-draw and additives

**Scope:** `classquiz/seed/banks/phleb/order_draw.py` (50 items, topic `phleb-order-draw`) and `classquiz/seed/banks/phleb/additives.py` (50 items, topic `phleb-additives`). Sources aligned with CLSI GP41 preanalytics, NHA CPT phlebotomy content, and OSHA/WHO injection-safety references where cited.

**Fact review:** Order-of-draw sequence (cultures first, citrate before serum/heparin/EDTA, gray last), discard-tube rationale, citrate fill ratio, syringe/butterfly transfer, culture sterility after hub contamination, needlestick reporting, and tube-additive/mechanism pairings were checked against standard teaching. No keyed answers were changed on factual grounds except minor wording trims for parallel options.

**1-in-5 / near-miss distractors:** Distractors emphasize confusable tube colors, reversed draw order, wrong additive for assay (EDTA vs citrate vs heparin vs fluoride), and plausible wrong actions (send underfilled citrate, top-off, relabel, attach late cultures). Joke or common-sense eliminators were not present in this pool.

**Bundle parity fixes (correct option listed multiple effects; distractors were single-clause):**
- **EDTA vigorous shake (order-draw):** Added a parallel hemolysis/platelet-clump near-miss distractor alongside clot-activator and no-mix myths.
- **Needlestick OSHA (order-draw):** Replaced single-step distractors with parallel multi-step wrong workflows (finish draw first, defer report, wait for symptoms).
- **Missed cultures after SST/lavender (order-draw):** Parallel distractors now bundle lab notification, same-needle bottles, cancel order, and SST-as-culture substitutions.
- **Syringe delay before citrate (order-draw):** Near-miss distractors cite air potency, “delay helps mixing,” and EDTA-only delay rules in parallel mechanism language.
- **SST gel function (additives):** Distractors now mirror the barrier concept with chelate/gel, broth-sterile, and heparin-to-citrate conversion near-misses.

**Other plausibility edits:**
- Light blue tube keyed answer expanded to **sodium citrate anticoagulant for coagulation testing** with parallel “anticoagulant for …” distractors (lavender/heparin/gray-oxalate confusion).
- 3.2% vs 3.8% citrate: removed **only** giveaway in stopper-color distractor; kept concentration/ratio near-misses.
- Late-culture corrective keyed answer shortened for clarity; distractors remain operationally tempting.
- Removed a few auto-length clauses that repeated “citrate, SST, and EDTA on one requisition” on unrelated stems; replaced with context-appropriate endings.

**Option length / banned qualifiers:** Correct answers trimmed where they were uniquely longest; shortest distractors extended with stem-hashed, assay-specific near-miss clauses (order-draw vs additives phrase pools). Distractors checked for banned `only` / `always` / `never` / `skip` qualifiers. Verified offline: **0** uniquely longest correct, **0** uniquely short correct by margin, **0** banned-qualifier hits in distractors for both files.

**Counts:** 100 items reviewed; substantive plausibility edits on **7** items; length/parallel balancing touched most items in the two files without changing keyed correctness.
